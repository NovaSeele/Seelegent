# Code of Conduct

Seelegent follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
This page is a short adaptation of it; where the two differ, the Contributor Covenant's text applies, with the
reporting channel given below.

## Our pledge

We want taking part in Seelegent to be free of harassment for everyone, whatever their age, body size, visible or
invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation. We act and speak in ways that keep this an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and healthy
community.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps:

- being kind and patient with other people;
- respecting different opinions, viewpoints and experiences;
- giving constructive feedback, and accepting it gracefully;
- taking responsibility for our mistakes, apologising to those affected and learning from them;
- focusing on what is best for the whole community, not only for ourselves.

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind;
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing other people's private information, such as a physical or e-mail address, without their explicit
  permission;
- other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement responsibilities

The maintainers clarify and enforce these standards and take fair corrective action in response to behaviour they
find inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful. They may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code,
issues and other contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct, and will explain why when that is
appropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all of the project's spaces (the repository, its issues, pull requests,
discussions and reviews) and when someone officially represents the project in public spaces.

## Reporting

Report behaviour that breaks this Code of Conduct privately: **open a private security advisory on GitHub** through
the repository's **Security** tab ([Report a vulnerability](https://github.com/NovaSeele/Seelegent/security/advisories/new)),
and say in its title that it is a Code of Conduct report. Only the maintainers can read it. Every report is reviewed
and investigated promptly and fairly, and the maintainers respect the privacy and security of the person who
reports.

## Enforcement guidelines

The maintainers follow the Contributor Covenant's community impact guidelines when they decide on consequences:

1. **Correction**, for inappropriate language or other behaviour that is unprofessional or unwelcome: a private
   written warning that explains what was wrong; a public apology may be requested.
2. **Warning**, for a single violation or a series of actions: a warning with consequences for continued behaviour,
   and no interaction with the people involved for a period of time.
3. **Temporary ban**, for a serious violation, including sustained inappropriate behaviour: a ban from any
   interaction with the community for a period of time.
4. **Permanent ban**, for a pattern of violations, harassment of an individual, or aggression towards or
   disparagement of groups of people: a permanent ban from any public interaction within the community.
